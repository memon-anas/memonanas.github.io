About

Curiosity built
this career.

I got into this field by accident. I was asked to fix a spreadsheet that had turned into a mess of broken formulas and duplicate entries, and instead of just patching it, I got curious about why it broke in the first place. That question turned into a job, and that job turned into 3.5+ years of building Airtable systems, APIs, and automation workflows for real businesses.

I started as a Junior developer, learning by doing: fixing bases, writing my first scripts, breaking things and figuring out why. Over time I moved into building systems from scratch, then into leading a team as Head, where my job shifted from writing every automation myself to helping others think through problems the same way I had learned to.

What hasn't changed is the curiosity. I still like sitting with a business process that doesn't work well and figuring out why, then building something that fixes it for good instead of patching it again next month. I learn new tools because a client's problem needs them, not because they're trending. That's led me into Airtable, Make.com, Zapier, n8n, and more recently AI integrations, always in service of the same goal: fewer manual steps, more reliable outcomes.

I like systems that disappear into the background. If I've done my job well, the people using what I build barely think about it, because it just works.

Automated Case Management

US-Based Law Firm Legal Process Automation 2+ Years
+

Problem

Case tracking, intake, and marketing lived across disconnected tools and spreadsheets. Cases fell through the cracks and reporting took days to compile.

Solution

A full case management platform built from the ground up, covering case tracking, sales and marketing, operations, KPI dashboards, and task management, with custom interfaces for each team.

Autonomous Quote Generation

Field Services Quote Automation 5 → 20 Quotes/Week
+

Problem

Quoting was entirely manual. Someone had to review job specs, work out pricing, and draft each quote by hand, which capped output at around 5 quotes a week no matter how much demand there was.

Solution

Built an automated quoting pipeline that pulls job details straight from Simpro, uses Claude to analyze scope and draft pricing, and routes the finished draft back for a quick human review instead of a full manual build.

Architecture

n8n orchestrates the workflow end to end, Simpro supplies job and pricing data, and Claude handles the analysis and drafting. The only manual step left is a final review, not the quote itself.

Impact

Quote output went from about 5 a week to 20, with the team reviewing finished drafts instead of building each one from scratch.

n8n Simpro Claude AI Quote Automation

Automated Invoicing & Payment Follow-Up

Finance Operations Billing Automation Faster Payment Cycles
+

Problem

Invoicing, payment reminders, and financial reporting were all handled by hand, which meant late follow-ups, inconsistent notifications, and hours spent pulling numbers together every month.

Solution

Built an automated system that generates and sends invoices as jobs close out, follows up with payment reminders and notifications on a set schedule, and rolls everything into a monthly report on its own.

Architecture

Xero handles invoice generation and payment tracking, Make.com runs the reminder and notification sequences, ChatGPT drafts the monthly report summaries, and Airtable keeps the underlying billing data organized.

Impact

Faster payment cycles, far fewer missed follow-ups, and monthly reports that used to take hours now show up on their own.

Xero Airtable Make.com ChatGPT

DM-to-Lead Qualification System

Marketing & Sales Lead Qualification Faster Response Time
+

Problem

Social DMs were a growing source of interest, but there was no consistent way to respond fast or tell which conversations were actually worth a follow-up.

Solution

Built an automated nurture flow that replies to DMs instantly, asks a few qualifying questions, and routes qualified conversations straight into the CRM for the sales team to work.

Architecture

ManyChat handles the conversation itself, Zapier passes qualified responses through, and Airtable holds the lead record and status the team actually works from.

Impact

Faster response times on social, and a clean, qualified pipeline instead of a pile of unsorted DMs.

ManyChat Zapier Airtable Lead Qualification

24/7 AI Appointment Setter

Operations Voice AI 24/7 Coverage
+

Problem

Missed calls meant missed leads, and booking an appointment still depended on someone being free to answer the phone and check the calendar in real time.

Solution

Built a voice AI system that answers inbound calls, talks the caller through availability, and books the appointment directly, with no human needed on the call.

Architecture

Retell AI handles the live phone conversation, and Make.com connects it to the calendar, checking real-time availability and confirming the booking automatically.

Impact

The line is covered 24 hours a day, interested leads get booked immediately instead of waiting on a callback, and the team only sees confirmed appointments.
